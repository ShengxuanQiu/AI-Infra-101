# Contributing to AI Infra 101

Thanks for helping keep this roadmap compact and high-signal.

## What we want

- concise technical corrections;
- better diagrams/formulas;
- high-quality first-party engineering blogs;
- strong GitHub notes that replace hours of passive course-watching;
- interview questions that test mechanisms and trade-offs;
- updates when vLLM/SGLang/Megatron architecture changes materially.

## What we usually do not want

- giant undifferentiated link dumps;
- generic MLOps/RAG/app-development content;
- copied third-party articles;
- claims such as “X is always compute-bound” without workload qualifiers;
- benchmark numbers without hardware/model/workload context.

## Resource contribution format

For each link, include:

```text
Title:
URL:
Layer:
What it teaches:
Why it is better/different from existing links:
Difficulty:
Last checked:
```

## Copyright

Prefer original summaries and links. Do not copy substantial third-party text/code into this repository unless the license clearly allows redistribution and the attribution/license requirements are satisfied.
